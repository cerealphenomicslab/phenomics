## Biography

Molecular biologist and bioinformatician. Since December 2025 he has been a postdoctoral researcher in the Cereal Phenomics Team at the Institute of Plant Genetics, Polish Academy of Sciences. He received his PhD from Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań in November 2025, after completing his engineering and master's degrees at the Warsaw University of Life Sciences (SGGW).

His research focuses on how plants respond to environmental stress at the genome and transcriptome levels. He combines sequencing data analysis (RNA-seq, including miRNAs and lncRNAs) with phenotyping, and in the team he works on barley drought response and the role of phytohormones in shaping root architecture. Previously he studied the population and evolutionary genetics of pines, and early in his career the conservation genetics of forest birds.

## Research interests

- Plant transcriptomics and population genomics
- Molecular mechanisms of plant responses to abiotic stress, especially drought in barley
- Regulatory networks and hormone signalling, including the role of phytohormones in root development
- Evolutionary genetics of pines: introgression, hybridisation and habitat adaptation
- Bioinformatics
