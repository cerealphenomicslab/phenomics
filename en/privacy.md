## Data controller

The controller of personal data processed in connection with this website is the **Institute of Plant Genetics, Polish Academy of Sciences**, Strzeszyńska 34, 60-479 Poznań, Poland. Contact details of the Institute and its Data Protection Officer are available at [www.igr.poznan.pl](https://www.igr.poznan.pl).

## Cookies and analytics

This website **does not use cookies** or analytics tools and does not track visitors. Fonts are served from our own server, not from third-party services.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub servers may log technical connection data, such as IP addresses, for security purposes. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Google Maps

On the *Contact* page the Google map loads only after you click “Show map”. Your browser then connects to Google servers, which may record data about your visit under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## External links

This website links to external services (e.g. Instagram, ORCID, ResearchGate, Google Scholar, journal publishers, the National Science Centre). Their own privacy policies apply once you follow those links.

## E-mail

If you e-mail us, we use your data (address and message) only to reply and correspond with you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).
