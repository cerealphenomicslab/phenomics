The Institute of Plant Genetics, Polish Academy of Sciences, is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with the Polish Act of 4 April 2019 on the digital accessibility of websites and mobile applications of public bodies. This statement applies to the **Cereal Phenomics Lab** website (Cereal Phenomics Team).

- Website publication date: [publication date – to be added after the domain goes live].
- Last major update: 18 November 2026.

## Compliance status

This website is **partially compliant** with WCAG 2.1 level AA due to the following:

- The map on the *Contact* page is provided by Google and may not be fully accessible. The address is also given as text.
- Some content is still being prepared: team photos are temporarily replaced by placeholder graphics, and some descriptions contain parts in square brackets to be completed.
- Publications and links lead to external services (publishers, ORCID, ResearchGate, Instagram) whose accessibility we do not control.

## Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 18 November 2026 on the basis of a self-assessment by the team.

## Accessibility features

- A “Skip to content” link at the top of every page.
- Full keyboard operation with a clearly visible focus indicator.
- Responsive layout and resizable text.
- Text colours meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements.
- Polish and English versions.

## Feedback and contact

If you encounter accessibility problems, please contact us at [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}) or [{{ site.contact.phone }}](tel:{{ site.contact.phone | remove: ' ' }}). You may also request information in an alternative format. Requests are handled within 7 days (in justified cases up to 2 months). If a request is refused, you may file a complaint and then apply to the [Polish Commissioner for Human Rights](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/).

## Physical accessibility

Information on the physical accessibility of the Institute building (Strzeszyńska 34, Poznań) is available in the Institute's accessibility statement at [www.igr.poznan.pl](https://www.igr.poznan.pl).
